<Chapter n="01" title="The week before.">

I was a few weeks from finishing my degree. The week before, I worked back-to-back waiting shifts: on my feet, carrying plates, the whole thing. Not exactly the prep you'd plan for a solo, day-long build.

I showed up anyway.

</Chapter>

<Chapter n="02" title="The brief.">

Build a contactless charity donation platform from scratch, in one day: a donor app, an admin dashboard, role-based access, a live transaction ledger and multi-currency support.

The organisers encouraged AI tools, so I paired with Copilot and checked what each change did before keeping it.

</Chapter>

<Chapter n="04" title="Where the day went.">

OAuth 1.0a with RSA-SHA256 signatures, .p12 keystores, and documentation that confidently described behaviour the sandbox didn't have.

The tap-to-pay guide was out of date. The parts of one feature were spread across pages that linked to more pages, and none of them told the whole story. I pieced them together into what I actually needed. The kind of problem that doesn't care how tired you are.

It took a while, but I got it working.

</Chapter>

<Chapter n="05" title="Next year.">

The organisers at ICE Campus ran a tight ship: a clear brief, good structure and fair judging. The kind of competition that makes you want to come back.

Next year I'm coming with a full night's sleep and zero pending orders.

</Chapter>
